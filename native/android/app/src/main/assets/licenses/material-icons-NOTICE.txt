The toolbar drawable icons are Google Material Icons. WordWarp converted the
upstream SVG paths into Android VectorDrawable XML with a monochrome tint;
these format/color adaptations do not change their third-party license.
Each source drawable records its original SVG URL.

Included drawables: ic_add, ic_canvas, ic_close, ic_edit, ic_effects,
ic_expand_more, ic_layers, ic_menu, ic_more, ic_pause, ic_play, ic_redo,
ic_remove, ic_styles, and ic_undo.

The WordWarp launcher logo is original project artwork and is not a Material Icon.
